Selected work

Four pieces of a larger portfolio — chosen because each one is defensible down to the number, not because they're the biggest. iMobile serves 1M+ customers across savings, cards, loans, deposits and servicing; everything below sits inside that.

iMobile · Direct system integration

Regulatory & servicing automation

A set of high-volume regulatory and servicing journeys ran through manual back-office intervention at every step — slow for customers, heavy on operations, and prone to rejection on incomplete or mismatched requests. I led direct system integration to automate the workflow end to end, including accessibility and language-access changes made to stay aligned with RBI requirements.

90% fewer manual ops touchpoints 80–90% lower rejection rate 11,000+ monthly requests

iMobile · 9+ journeys consolidated

Unifying fragmented servicing journeys

Customer servicing was scattered across disparate platforms — the same request could start in one place and finish in another, with no single owner. I consolidated nine-plus of these flows into seamless journeys inside iMobile, cutting cross-channel redundancy and giving customers one consistent path.

+5 NPS points 5,000+ monthly requests

iMobile · Ground-up enablement

UPI access, from zero

A specific customer segment had no path to instant payments — UPI simply wasn't available to them. I scoped and shipped access from the ground up, working through the constraints that had kept the segment underserved, without touching the experience for the existing user base.

10,000+ users unblocked Zero disruption to existing base

WhatsApp Banking · Customer engagement

WhatsApp Banking campaigns

As sole PM on WhatsApp Banking, I built and ran targeted customer engagement campaigns and the servicing journeys behind them — reaching several hundred thousand customers per campaign and designing the conversational flow that converts a message into a completed action.

300K–400K reached per campaign 80% conversion on targeted campaigns
