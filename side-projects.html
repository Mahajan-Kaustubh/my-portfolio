Omnitable

A gesture-controlled smart dashboard, embedded into a tabletop surface — built to solve a problem I actually had: critical daily information scattered across too many apps and devices.

What it does

Omnitable consolidates health tracking, financial portfolio monitoring, commute intelligence, calendar and productivity data into a single ambient interface — eliminating the need to context-switch across separate apps just to see the shape of your day.

How it's built

I defined the product vision, UX architecture and system design end to end, then integrated third-party data sources — Strava for activity, Todoist for tasks, and live financial market feeds — into gesture-based interactions on an embedded computer running beneath the table surface.
